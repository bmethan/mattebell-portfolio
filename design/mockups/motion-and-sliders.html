02 Working indicators

Your reference turns out to be 3D in disguise: when the square collapses to a diagonal line, that is a square turned edge-on. These are real 3D rotations, drawn with depth shading (nearer points brighter). Try the glyph and color options.

1. Pivot

Closest to your reference. The square turns 180 degrees about one diagonal, then the other.

2. XYZ cube

At rest it is the same four dots. Mid-turn the hidden back corners appear: it is a cube, turning a quarter about X, then Y, then Z.

3. Axis gizmo

The viewport gizmo from every DCC, stepping a quarter turn at a time like a turntable. Red, green, blue is the industry convention; teal keeps it on brand.
